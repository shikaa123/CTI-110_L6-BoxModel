The Characters

  1. Na Hwa-Jin
    • Played by Kim Mu-Yeol
    • He is our main character. He is a former military special ops operative who works as an inspector for the ERPB.
    • Traumatized, very traumatized. But funny!
  2. Im Han-rim
    • Played by Jin Ki-Joo
    • A fearless, loose-cannon ERPB inspector and former military colleague of Hwa-jin who handles tough disciplinary cases.
  3. Bong Geun-Dae
    • Played by Pyo Ji-hoon
    • The Brilliant, tech-savvy "brains" of operations for the ERPB

The Plot

Set in modern day South Korea, the main character, Na Hwa-Jin, goes to different problematic schools and sets them straight. He, along with his two fellow inspectors, Bong Geun-Dae and Im Han-Rim, solve cases and help innocent teachers and students alike. While all of this happens, the main storyline, including Na Hwa-Jin, Minister Choi Gang-Seok, and the Ministers daughter, (also Hwa-Jins fiance) continues over the course of 10 episodes. Long story short, his fiance, a great teacher, was attacked by a student. The student got away with only 1 year in juvenile detention. However, something dark is brewing in the background. They face lots of criticism and terrible people, but get through it, and improve the school system greatly.

Note

As an avid consumer of multiple kinds of media, this show was very captivating! Every episode was as interesting as the last one, and there was never a dull moment. It also taught me lot, and had lots of inspirational words that a person could use in their own life. I personally would consider this a really good show for anyone who wanted something new to watch.

Random Fact!

Some of the stories in this series are based off of real events! Specifically, episodes 3, 5, 6, and 9.
